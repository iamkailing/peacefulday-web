  • Cold water immersion may not be suitable for people with heart conditions, uncontrolled high blood pressure, or during pregnancy. When booking, you confirm that you and your guests are fit to take part, or have checked with a doctor.
  • Please follow our team's instructions at the venue, and tell them straight away if you feel unwell.

Packages and gift vouchers

  • Each package or voucher shows how many sessions it includes and how long it's valid. Use the code when you book.
